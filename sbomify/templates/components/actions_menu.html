{% comment %} The row and header actions menu: the "meatball" trigger and the panel it opens. The items are the slot, not a list prop, because real menus mix links, HTMX buttons and Alpine dispatches. Write them with c-dropdowns.item, c-dropdowns.item-danger, c-dropdowns.header and c-dropdowns.divider; the panel itself is c-dropdowns.dropdown in its floating form, so this file owns the trigger, the open state and the positioning and restates none of their recipes. The menu is teleported to the body rather than positioned inside its row: a data table scrolls horizontally, which clips on both axes, so a menu opened from the last row was cut off at the container's edge. The actionsMenu Alpine component positions it against the trigger and closes it on any scroll, since a fixed panel does not follow a scrolling ancestor. stretch makes the trigger as tall as the control beside it, for the action row of a page header. The two branches below differ only in the trigger's shape and must stay in lockstep: they cannot be one call because the stretch recipe replaces the size one rather than adding to it. {% endcomment %}
