Each segment grows by its count, so the bar reads as a ratio at a glance and needs no axis. segments is a list of {tone, count}, zero counts already dropped; tone is one of success, info, warning, danger or neutral. label is the written breakdown ("6 passed, 1 failed") and is what a screen reader hears, so colour is never the only carrier. With no segments the empty track still renders, which keeps a column of rows aligned when one of them has nothing to measure.
