Five bands would be five near-identical files and pages interpolate the band they were given, so the band is a level prop: critical, high, medium, low, and unknown for anything off the scale. The shape comes from the shell (shape="severity"): squarer and uppercase, so the shape carries the "this is a severity" signal, not just the colour. The text mixes the accent toward the theme text colour, because amber and cyan on their own tint fail contrast in both themes. {% endcomment %} {{ slot }}
