{% comment %} A small status word: a severity, a state, or a label the workspace owns. Two recipes, not seven. A tone badge is a tint of its colour behind text of that colour; a brand badge is a solid fill under the measured ink. Which colour a tone uses is the only thing that varies, so it arrives as --tone in the style attribute and the utilities below name it once. Six near-identical colour-mix branches was the alternative, and that is how a ramp drifts: one branch gets a tweak and the others do not. The severity ramp is the platform's own, straight from the tokens. It is not brandable and must not be: critical has to read as critical on every trust centre, whatever colour the workspace picked. Anything a workspace legitimately owns takes brand instead, which is a solid fill because a pale brand as text on its own tint is invisible. An unknown tone falls through to neutral rather than rendering an unstyled badge, so a status the model gains later is quiet instead of broken. {% endcomment %} {{ slot }}
