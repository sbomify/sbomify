{% comment %} The callout that explains why something is not visible: an artifact behind an access request, a document behind an NDA. It is the one branded component whose job is to stop the reader, so it takes the full width and leads with an icon. The tint is the brand at low alpha rather than a warning colour. Being asked to sign in is not an error, and painting it amber makes a normal step look like a failure. The icon sits on a solid brand chip rather than being brand-coloured itself: an icon tinted with a pale brand disappears against the panel behind it. The panel keeps the low-alpha tint, because the text on it is neutral. The action slot is where the caller puts the button that resolves it, so the notice never has to know what the next step is. {% endcomment %}
