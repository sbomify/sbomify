{% comment %} The top of a branded page: what this page is, in one line, with room for a short explanation and whatever the page needs beside it. It is a separate component from c-branded.section-header rather than one component with a size prop. A page header and a section heading are different things, not two sizes of the same thing: they sit in different places, carry different weight, and only one of them ever holds actions or figures. A prop that switches between them would let a page put a page header halfway down itself, which is exactly the drift this library exists to prevent. The title is a real h1 because it is the page's name. Nothing here is branded: a workspace's colour on a heading has to survive being read at 30px against the page, and pale brands do not. The eyebrow slot is where a brand mark belongs if one is wanted, since a small solid chip is safe at any brand. lede is optional, and the slot beneath takes anything the page leads with, such as a figures strip. {% endcomment %}
