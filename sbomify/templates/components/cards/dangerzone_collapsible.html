{% comment %} The Danger Zone, assembled: the red band that is also the control that opens it, and the collapsing body the destructive rows go in. Every page's danger zone composes this one component, so the band, the chevron and the keyboard behaviour cannot drift apart per page. They did once, three ways: the team zone sat on c-cards.collapsible level="danger", which leaves the title and chevron in neutral ink, and the pages that did use the dangerzone parts had hand-copied two different header arrangements. state names the caller's boolean that is true while the zone is open. It defaults to isExpanded, which is what dangerZone() in alpine-components.ts provides, so most callers pass nothing. The trigger flips it by assignment, the way c-modal derives its dismiss from show, so a caller supplies state and never a handler. The chevron matches c-cards.collapsible-trigger: down while shut, turned over while open, on the right edge of the band. One collapsing motion across the app, danger or not. The rows are the slot, landing on c-cards.dangerzone-body so the collapse sits on the padded element itself; the confirm modal a row opens belongs to the caller, next to this tag, because only the caller knows what it deletes. {% endcomment %}
