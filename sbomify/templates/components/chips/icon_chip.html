{% comment %} The accent carrier of the design language: a flat 12% tint of the accent with the accent as foreground. Every "icon beside a heading" is one of these, from stat cards to modal headers to empty states. The slot is the icon. Size and radius are two conditional segments, so circle replaces the radius rather than stacking a second one on top of the size's. accent names a token (neutral, info, success, warning, danger). With no accent the chip reads --chip-accent from itself or an ancestor and falls back to primary, so a one-off tint is still style="--chip-accent: var(--color-accent-pink)". {% endcomment %} {{ slot }}
