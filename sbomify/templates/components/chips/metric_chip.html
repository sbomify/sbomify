{% comment %} One pill of the stats strip under a page header: an icon, a label and a value on a bordered surface. The parts go in the slot as c-chips.metric.label and c-chips.metric.value, because a chip reads "12 Components" or "Scanned 3h ago" depending on the metric, and the icon is the caller's own. accent names a token (info, success, warning, danger) and renders as one conditional segment carrying the fill, the border colour and the text colour together, so no two of them ever land on the same chip. The label stays muted under every accent; the value and the icon take it. The line-height is stated because the chip's height is its value's line box: text-sm alone brings 1.25rem where the old rule inherited 1.5, which took a pixel off every chip and off the strip they sit in. {% endcomment %} {{ slot }}
