{% comment %} One pill of the stats strip under a page header: an icon, a label and a value on a bordered surface. The parts go in the slot as c-chips.metric.label and c-chips.metric.value, because a chip reads "12 Components" or "Scanned 3h ago" depending on the metric, and the icon is the caller's own. accent names a token (info, success, warning, danger) and renders as one conditional segment carrying the fill, the border colour and the text colour together, so no two of them ever land on the same chip. The icon, a decorative mark, takes the accent itself. The text is read on a tint of the accent, where the accent and muted grey both fall below 4.5:1 on a light surface, so the segment also hands the parts --metric-ink, the accent's ink, for the value, and --metric-label, full text, for the label. The parts wrap inside the chip on narrow screens; the chip never widens the page to keep a scan summary on one line. The line-height is stated because the chip's height is its value's line box: text-sm alone brings 1.25rem where the old rule inherited 1.5, which took a pixel off every chip and off the strip they sit in. {% endcomment %} {{ slot }}
