The slot sits under the subtitle, for the rare header that carries a badge or a note of its own. The named actions slot holds the control the section is headed by, on the right: "Add entity" over a list of entities. The title block only takes the free space when there is something to push against, so a header with no actions still sizes itself to its text and wraps where it always did. {% endcomment %}
