{% comment %} A heading over a bulleted list that folds past `limit` items. Built for an advisory's affected releases, which once ran as one comma-joined line inside a chip and off the side of the sidebar when an advisory named a few dozen. One item per line keeps long identifiers readable, and folding keeps a long list from pushing the rest of the column out of view. The slot is the heading. `items` is a list of strings, `limit` how many show before the toggle (default 5), `noun` what the count beside the heading counts (default "item"), `label` the list's accessible name. {% endcomment %}
