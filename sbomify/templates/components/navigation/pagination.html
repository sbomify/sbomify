Pass show_prev_next="" for a numbers-only pager. Every cell is a c-navigation.page-link, so the pager owns the row and nothing else, and each cell is rendered `only`, on its own props, so nothing the pager holds (its class, above all) can fall through into it. {% endcomment %}
