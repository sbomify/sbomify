{% comment %} One step on a stepper rail: the circle and its caption. state is where the flow has got to (completed, active, or neither), and it renders as one conditional segment on the circle, so a step never carries two fills. The slot is what goes in the circle, a number or a tick; label is the caption beside it, which the active step brightens. label_class is layout for that caption, and only for it: a long rail hides its captions on a narrow screen (hidden sm:inline) while the circles stay. The caption states no display of its own, so nothing there stacks. {% endcomment %}
