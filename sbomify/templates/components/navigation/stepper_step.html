The slot is what goes in the circle, a number or a tick; label is the caption beside it, which the active step brightens. label_class is layout for that caption, and only for it: a long rail hides its captions on a narrow screen (hidden sm:inline) while the circles stay. The caption states no display of its own, so nothing there stacks. compact uses a small neutral dot for setup flows.
